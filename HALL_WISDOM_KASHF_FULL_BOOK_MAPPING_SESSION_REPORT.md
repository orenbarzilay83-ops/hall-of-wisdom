## Number of dhamir methods

**17, structured as 8 core + 9 additional**: the previously-known
5-type/8-sub-method core structure (re-verified exactly against the new
source this round, no discrepancies in the book's own description), plus
**9 additional, separately-named, fully-described techniques found at
p.156-160** that are explicitly outside the core 5-type selection — the
book's own author states at p.151 that he presents "five types from
among" many ways of revealing hidden intent, which this round's reading
confirmed is a literal, not rhetorical, statement. Several of the 9
additional techniques share a "sum points, reduce by modulus N, cast
onto houses" family resemblance (moduli 8, 9, 12 recur) rather than
being 9 wholly independent algorithms — this nuance is preserved in the
Sub-Engine Inventory rather than collapsed into a flat count.
