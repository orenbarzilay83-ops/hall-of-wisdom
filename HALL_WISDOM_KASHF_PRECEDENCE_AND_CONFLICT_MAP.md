## Summary

- **6 witness systems (A-F)** exist as independently-described mechanisms; **no cross-system reconciliation rule was found** (§5.2) — this is the single largest, most consequential unresolved precedence gap in the entire mapping.
- **17+ dhamir techniques** (8 core sub-methods + 9 additional) have exactly one stated aggregation rule (majority, §7.1), and even that rule's scope (which subset of techniques it applies to) is not fully specified.
- The book **does** resolve some of its own internal disputes explicitly (Order 4's planet assignment, §10.2; Order 2's seat exception, §11.1) and **does not** resolve others (Chapter 5's gender methods, §10.3; the 4 element-value traditions' exact numeric bridge, §10.1).
- **No topic-formula-vs-witness conflict rule, no primary-vs-alt-formula tie-breaker, and no universal calculation-order pipeline were found anywhere in the source** — these three gaps (§4.2, §6.1, §12.4) are the most actionable "unresolved" items for any future engine-design conversation, precisely because they are the questions any implementation would be forced to answer one way or another.
- **No "completer" concept distinct from the judge (house 15) exists in Kashf** as read this round (§9.3) — this should inform how the Sub-Engine Inventory's "judge/completer" category is understood going forward: in Kashf specifically, there is one role, not two.
- Nothing in this document invents a resolution where the source is silent — every `unresolved` row above is a genuine textual gap, not an extraction failure, confirmed by full sequential reading rather than keyword search.
